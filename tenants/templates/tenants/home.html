Why Kuraimi

Built for the MENA region

Most ERPs are built for western markets and retrofitted for Arabic. Kuraimi was designed from day one for bilingual, RTL-first enterprises.

  • Full Arabic RTL Support

    Native right-to-left layout with Arabic, English, and Indonesian. Switch languages in one click — no page reload.

  • Multi-Tenant Cloud

    Each company gets its own isolated database. Your data never mixes with other tenants. Fully cloud-hosted with 99.9% SLA.

  • AI-Powered Insights

    Built-in machine learning for revenue forecasting, anomaly detection, and demand planning using Prophet and scikit-learn.

  • Mobile App Included

    Flutter mobile app for field teams. Access dashboards, approve leave, check inventory, and manage tasks on the go.
