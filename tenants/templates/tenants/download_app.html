Features

Everything you need at your fingertips

Designed for employees who are always on the move — fast, intuitive, and works in Arabic and English.

Attendance & Clock-In

Clock in and out from your phone, scan QR codes at kiosks, and view your full attendance history with worked hours and overtime tracking.

Leave Requests

Submit, track, and cancel leave requests in seconds. See your leave balance, pending approvals, and full history all in one place.

Payslips & Salary

View and download your monthly payslips instantly. See a clear breakdown of your earnings, deductions, allowances, and net pay.

Tasks & Projects

Stay on top of your assigned tasks. Update statuses, leave comments, and track progress without needing to be at your desk.

Point of Sale

For sales-floor employees: open POS sessions, process orders, accept payments, and print receipts directly from the app.

Dashboard & Insights

A personalized dashboard showing your schedule, pending leave requests, unread notifications, and task summary — the moment you open the app.

Installation

Get started in 3 steps

No app store required. Install directly from this page in under a minute.

1

Download the APK

Tap the Download button above to save the APK file to your Android device.

2

Allow Installation

Go to Settings → Security → enable "Install from unknown sources" (or allow it when prompted).

3

Log In

Open the app and log in with the credentials your HR team provided. Select your company database and you're in.
