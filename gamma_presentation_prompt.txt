# Slide 2: Background & Context

- **The Rise of ML:** Modern businesses want Machine Learning (ML) for demand forecasting, credit risk, and employee retention.
- **The "Black-Box" Problem:** Embedded ML models give predictions without explanations. If business users don't understand the "why" behind the AI, they cannot trust or act safely on the decisions.

# Slide 3: Problem Statement
- **High Costs & Rigid Architectures:** SMEs cannot afford single-tenant ERPs.
- **Missing Core Modules:** SMEs need accessible POS (Point of Sale) and CRM (Customer Relationship Management) workflows.
- **Opacity of AI:** ML predictions lack transparency. 
- **Hidden Risks:** Standard metrics (like AUC-ROC) can mask "data leakage" (when a model cheats using data it shouldn't have), leading to failed real-world deployment.

# Slide 4: Proposed Solution
- **A Lightweight, Multi-Tenant ERP:** Built specifically for small enterprise implementation.
- **Cost-effective:** Shared infrastructure using database-per-tenant architecture.
- **Rich Business Modules:** Contains 9 core modules including Finance, CRM, POS, HR, and Inventory.
- **Embedded Explainable AI (XAI):** Uses SHAP and LIME methodologies to open the "black box" of machine learning, making predictions transparent and auditable.

# Slide 5: Research Objectives
- Design and implement a full-stack, web-based ERP system for SMEs.
- Establish a multi-tenant PostgreSQL structure for secure data isolation.
- Integrate ML models for business intelligence (e.g., Late Payment Prediction, Lead Scoring, Employee Attrition).
- **Primary Goal:** Implement an XAI module (SHAP & LIME) to provide transparent feature attributions to non-technical users.
- Evaluate XAI as a *model validation tool* to catch data-quality issues and data leakage.

# Slide 6: System Architecture & Tech Stack
- **Backend Framework:** Django 4.2 (Python)

- **Development Methodology:** Agile (8 Sprints)

- **XAI Layer:** 
  - `shap.TreeExplainer` for exact Shapley-value feature importance.
  - `lime.TabularExplainer` for model-agnostic cross-validation.

# Slide 7: Explainable AI Methodology
- **Instance-Level Explanations:** Answering "Why was *this specific* invoice flagged as high-risk?" (Rendered as Waterfall plots).
- **Global Feature Importance:** Answering "What generally drives late payments across all customers?" (Rendered as Beeswarm plots).
- **Triangulation:** Using both SHAP (game-theory based) and LIME (local linear approximation) to ensure explanation robustness.

# Slide 8: Early Findings & Model Evaluation

  - Attrition was relying 55.9% on "payroll_months" (a proxy for the target).
